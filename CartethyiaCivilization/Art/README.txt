Cartethyia Civilization V0.2 art assets

- The leader portrait is based on a user-supplied official transparent
  Cartethyia image for this non-commercial fan mod.
- The civilization emblem is original AI-assisted art generated for this
  project.
- Source PNG files live in Art/Source.
- Civ5 DDS atlases and the Dawn of Man image live in Art/Atlases.
- Tools/build_art.py rebuilds every DDS size from the source PNG files.
- The unit model and diplomacy scene still reuse base-game assets.
